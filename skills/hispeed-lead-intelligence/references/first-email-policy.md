# First email policy

The draft is a researched reference for review, not authorization to send.

## Required outputs

- three concise subject options;
- one recommended subject;
- one primary English email;
- `evidence_used` with evidence IDs;
- `reason_for_angle`;
- any inference or claim requiring review.

## Leader-approved body sequence

### 1. Personalized Problem

Open with a problem, constraint, or opportunity specific to the prospect's observed business. When the need is inferred, use exploratory language such as “teams handling…” or “you may be evaluating…”. Never state an unverified pain, installed machine, budget, or project as fact.

### 2. Show You Solve It

Connect that problem to one relevant Hispeed capability. Explain the practical fit rather than listing the product catalog. Use only approved company facts and prospect evidence. Do not invent performance figures, customer cases, prices, delivery times, certification coverage, or warranty terms.

### 3. CTA

End with one low-friction next step: confirm relevance, identify the correct owner, share a part photo or specification, consider sample testing, or schedule a short discussion. One email has one CTA.

## Style

- Default body length: 80–130 English words unless the brief says otherwise.
- Use clear business English and one concrete personalization signal.
- Do not use fake `Re:`, false familiarity, clickbait, generic praise, or a product dump.
- Do not add an attachment or a stack of links by default.
- A subject may be interesting without making a claim the evidence cannot support.
