# Market targeting & query strategy

## Segments (do not mix without user approval)
A. **Distributor / reseller (pilot first)**: already sells industrial laser marking/engraving systems and/or complementary industrial identification machinery. Possible channel/OEM opportunity, not an established desire for supplier change. Ideal roles: Owner, Managing Director, Product Manager, Sales Director; Purchasing Manager if relevant.
B. **System integrator / machine builder**: builds traceability stations, industrial automation cells, permanent-ID integration. Roles: Engineering Director, Automation Manager, Technical Director, Project Manager.
C. **Industrial end user**: factory with documented part identification needs (auto parts, electrical/electronics, hardware, sensors, precision components). Roles: Process/Manufacturing Engineer, Production Manager, Plant Manager.

## Search construction
Use one market per batch, local language and English, 4–8 queries varied across product, channel, vertical and application. Templates (replace placeholders):
- `industrial fiber laser marking distributor {country}`
- `laser marking machine dealer {country}`
- `industrial marking systems reseller {local_language_keyword}`
- `laser traceability workstation integrator {country}`
- `direct part marking automation solutions {country}`
- `UV laser marking equipment supplier {country}`
- `site:{local_country_tld} laser marking distributor`
- targeted exhibition exhibitor lists / dealer locators / industry directories, then **verify the company website**.

## Exclusions
- DIY-only hobby laser shops with no credible industrial activity, consumer gifts/personalized retail services, generic marketing agencies, anonymous listing sites, irrelevant machinery, aggregator pages without identifiable domain.
- Exclude or manual-review existing customers, existing discussions, do-not-contact, territory protection, company subsidiaries under another name, and local brand conflict.
- Do not create false buying-intent scores from web browsing alone.

## Execution priorities
1. High-confidence active distributor with independently verifiable industrial laser marking catalog.
2. Integrator with clear machine-vision, serial/QR marking or part traceability projects.
3. End manufacturer only with an evidenced marking/traceability or relevant production process, not merely 'works with metal'.
