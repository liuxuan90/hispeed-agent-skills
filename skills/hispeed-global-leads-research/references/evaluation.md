# Evaluation / release checklist

Before releasing any new `stable` rules manifest:
1. Run a 5-company pilot in one user-approved market and segment.
2. Check all five company identities and official domain URLs; note absent confirmations.
3. Inspect source evidence for every claim about products and industry.
4. Evaluate named contacts / actual roles separately from work email validity.
5. Verify no lead was represented as `VALID` solely from a guessed email pattern.
6. Evaluate all email drafts for genuinely different evidence-based opening, subject, and one CTA.
7. Review compliance/channel exclusion, inability to write external systems, and duplicates.
8. Compare V1 vs proposed version metrics: confirmed firms / researched firms, acceptable ICPs / confirmed firms, contact identity confirmation, verified email share, manual editing time, positive reply rate (only after lawful actual sends).
9. Require an owner to approve version publication; publish to protected `stable` branch, update `rules-manifest.json` checksums.
10. Keep previous manifest and last good local rule set available for rollback. Never auto-publish unreviewed AI suggestions.
