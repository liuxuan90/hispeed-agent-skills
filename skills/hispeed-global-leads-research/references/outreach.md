# Personalized outreach (draft only)

## Style
Natural, concise, credible B2B industrial English; avoid automation-sounding compliments, unfounded pain-point claims, superlatives, and generic lists of laser models. Typical first email: 80–120 English words, subject ideally 4–9 words. One application/cooperation angle, one easy call to action. Never send from this Skill.

## Required research-to-email link
- Extract 1–2 **facts** about the prospect backed by source URLs; cite sources in the **internal rationale**, not usually in the email.
- Choose a relevant Hispeed marking/engraving solution as **possible fit**; avoid promise of performance without sample testing.
- Explain Chinese rationale: subject choice, why that application/product, what remains hypothetical and what data is missing.
- When contact name unknown, use neutral greeting. No invented first names.
- When only weak evidence exists, mark `DRAFT_REQUIRES_RESEARCH` and do not fake personalization.

## Example — entirely fictional company, not a real lead
Research fact (fictional): company sells industrial traceability stations and publishes Data Matrix application examples.
Subject: `Laser marking for traceability projects`
Email:
Hello [First Name],

I came across your company's industrial traceability solutions, particularly the use of Data Matrix identification in production workflows.

I'm with Hispeed Laser, a manufacturer of industrial laser marking and engraving equipment. We work with businesses evaluating marking systems for component identification and integration projects. Depending on the parts and line setup, a Fiber or UV marking configuration may be worth discussing.

Would a short overview of our marking solutions, along with sample marking examples, be useful for your team?

Best regards,
Hispeed Laser

Chinese rationale: 用公开证据（其追溯系统产品）切入；Fiber/UV 只是可能方案，尚未知道材料、节拍和系统集成要求。不得把示例当作实际发送记录。

## Follow-ups
This Skill may draft optional follow-up ideas **after** reply status is provided, but it cannot access mail or schedule sending by itself. Sending/opt-out approval remains downstream.
