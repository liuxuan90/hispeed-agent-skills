# Lead qualification (0–100; research fit only)

Score only evidence; use 0 for a component without supporting evidence rather than inventing information.

| Component | Max | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Product / business fit | 35 | Verified industrial laser marking/engraving portfolio or strongly evidenced direct part marking requirement |
| Channel / ICP fit | 25 | Confirmed industrial distributor / integrator / relevant manufacturer, appropriate to campaign |
| Application signal / business activity | 20 | Documented product portfolio, relevant project, case study or actual application; NOT buying intent |
| Decision-maker relevance | 10 | Verifiable current relevant role and company |
| Contact data completeness | 10 | Relevant person with verified-valid work email and documentation |

- `A` = 80–100; `B` = 65–79; `C` = <65. Use as internal priority ranking only.
- Forced hold regardless of numeric score: blocked/existing prospect without permission, exclusive channel territory conflict, unsubscribed, suspicious domain, no verifiable company, email invalid, unsupported email permission.
- Verification pending: a company may be scored A for research but **cannot be auto-approved for sending**.
- Report each component score and 2–3 sentences of justification with evidence links.
- Do not conflate `application_match` with proven purchasing activity.
